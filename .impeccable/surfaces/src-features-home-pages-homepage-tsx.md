Scope: Public homepage at `/`; mode: Persuade.

Audience: guests comparing a stay and deciding whether to search available
rooms. Job: understand the offer, trust the availability/pricing workflow, and
start a dated room search. Primary action: search available rooms. Supporting
proof is limited to real Backend room data and verified product behavior; no
invented ratings, reviews, awards, booking counts, room sizes, or policies.

Direction: Calm Arrival Journey. An immersive hospitality image and compact
booking rail form the first viewport. The scroll moves through verified
clarity, an asymmetric room selection, a lifestyle chapter, a property-image
gallery, and a decisive close. The memorable moment is the search rail anchored
to the photographic threshold, making inspiration and action one composition.

Constraints: Vietnamese copy, responsive desktop/tablet/mobile, keyboard and
reduced-motion support, and no duplication of Backend availability logic. Until
approved property photography exists, marketing imagery must be clearly labelled
as illustrative; room facts, prices, and links continue to come from the Backend.
